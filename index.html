How these tools work

Everything runs in your browser

Names, lists and anything else you type stay on your device. The tools are plain JavaScript that runs on the page, so nothing you enter is sent to a server or saved after you leave.

Fair randomness

Every name, team slot and coin side has the same chance. Teams are built with a standard Fisher–Yates shuffle and names are drawn evenly from the whole list, so where someone sits in the list doesn't matter. No weighting, no hidden favorites.

Free, with ads

There are no accounts, trials or paid tiers. The site is paid for by a few ads, kept below the tools so they never get between you and the answer.
