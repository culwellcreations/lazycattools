Convert recipes between cups and grams for the ingredient you're actually using, not a one-size-fits-all number. Free, and everything runs in your browser.

Why "1 cup = X grams" depends on the ingredient

A cup measures volume; a scale measures weight. A cup of all-purpose flour weighs about 120 g, a cup of granulated sugar about 200 g, and a cup of butter about 227 g. Even the same ingredient varies: flour scooped straight from the bag can weigh 20–30% more than flour spooned into the cup and leveled. That's why online answers disagree, and why each converter here is set up for one specific ingredient and says how it was measured.
