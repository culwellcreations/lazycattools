Your Teams

How to Use the Random Team Generator

Enter your names, one per line or separated by commas, choose how many teams you want, and tap Generate Teams. Reshuffle gives you a new split, Share Link sends the same teams to your group, and Print gives you a paper copy for the gym wall or the classroom board.

How uneven groups get split

The tool shuffles the whole list, then deals the names out to the teams one at a time, like dealing cards. That keeps every team within one person of the others:

  • 10 people in 3 teams: 4, 3 and 3
  • 23 people in 4 teams: 6, 6, 6 and 5
  • 30 people in 7 teams: two teams of 5 and five teams of 4

If you'd rather think in group sizes (say, groups of 4), divide your headcount by the size you want and round down to get the number of teams. 26 students in groups of 4 is 6 teams: four groups of 4 and two groups of 5.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work with uneven numbers?

Yes. Extra people are added one per team, so some teams end up one person bigger. No team is ever empty.

How many teams can I make?

Anywhere from 2 to 10. You need at least as many names as teams.

Can I upload a list instead of typing?

Yes. Use Upload .txt or .csv to load a file with one name per line or names separated by commas.

What are random team names?

Turn on Random team names to give each team a generated name instead of Team 1 and Team 2. It helps at trivia nights, and kids like having a team name to cheer for.

Is this tool free?

Yes. No account, no sign-up, and no limit on how many times you shuffle.

Who uses a random team generator?

Teachers making groups, coaches picking sides for a scrimmage, trivia hosts splitting tables, and anyone running a game night who wants the teams to be fair.
