        How to Use the Secret Santa Generator

        Type or paste everyone's name, one per line. Add a spending limit and exchange date if you want them on everyone's draw. If anyone shouldn't draw each other, open Keep people apart and add the pair. Then tap Draw Names.

        Now get the draws to people. Tap Copy link next to each name and send that link to that person by text or email. When they open it, they see their own draw and nobody else's. If everyone's in the same room, skip the links: pass your phone around and have each person tap their own name, look, and tap Hide before handing it on. Print slips gives you one slip per person to fold and hand out.

        How the private links work

        Each link carries one person's draw in the part of the address after the #. Browsers never send that part to a website, so the draw goes from your device to theirs without passing through ours. Nothing is stored, and there's no account to make.

        The links are private, not locked. Anyone who opens a link sees that draw, so send each link only to its person. If you're the organizer and you're also in the exchange, just don't open the other links yourself.

        How to run a Secret Santa

        1. Set a spending limit. $20 to $30 is typical for coworkers and $25 to $50 for family. A clear number keeps one person from showing up with a $5 mug while someone else spent $60.
        2. Pick the exchange date. Give people at least two weeks, more if gifts need to ship.
        3. Collect names and the people to keep apart. Couples and housemates usually sit out drawing each other.
        4. Draw and send the links. Ask everyone to reply once they've opened theirs, so you know nobody's link went to spam.
        5. Keep it secret until the day. If someone loses their draw, you can open their link again without seeing anyone else's.

        Frequently Asked Questions

        Can someone draw their own name?

        No. Every draw is checked before you see it. Nobody gets themselves, and everyone gives exactly one gift and gets exactly one.

        How do I keep couples from drawing each other?

        Open Keep people apart, pick the two names and tap Add. It works both ways: neither person can draw the other. Add as many pairs as you need.

        Can the organizer join the exchange too?

        Yes. Send everyone their private link and don't open the other links yourself. You only see your own draw, the same as everyone else.

        Is my list uploaded anywhere?

        No. The draw happens in your browser and nothing is saved. Each private link carries one person's draw in the part of the address after the #, which browsers don't send to the website. Close the tab and the list is gone.

        Is the draw fair?

        Yes. The names are shuffled with your browser's secure random number generator, and any shuffle that breaks a rule (someone drawing themselves, or a pair you kept apart) is thrown out and reshuffled. Every valid draw is equally likely.

        How many people can I add?

        From 3 to 100. Each name has to be different, so add a last initial if two people share a first name.

        Why does it say no valid draw is possible?

        Your exclusions leave someone with nobody they're allowed to buy for. It happens most with small groups: with 3 people, keeping any two apart makes a draw impossible. Remove an exclusion or add a person and draw again.
