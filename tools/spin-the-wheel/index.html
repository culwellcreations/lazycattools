    How to Use Spin the Wheel

    Type or paste your entries, one per line, and the wheel redraws as you go. Tap Spin or the middle of the wheel. When it stops, the winner shows under the wheel. Remove and spin again takes the winner off and spins straight away, which is how you go through a list without repeats. Reset wheel puts everything you removed back.

    For a classroom or a game night, tap Fullscreen and put it on the projector or TV. Copy link to this wheel gives you a link with your entries in it, so you can send the same wheel to someone else or save it for next week.

    How the spin picks a winner

    The winner is chosen the moment you tap Spin, using your browser's secure random number generator, and every slice has the same chance. The wheel then turns five to seven times and slows down onto that slice. The animation is for show; the odds were settled before it started. Duplicates count: an entry on the list twice has two slices and twice the chance.

    Everything happens on your device. Your entries aren't sent anywhere or saved, and they're gone when you close the tab.

    Ways to use it

    • Calling on students. Load the class list, spin, and remove each student once they've answered.
    • Game night. Put the games on the wheel and stop arguing about it.
    • Dinner. Try the restaurant list, or type in the places you'd actually go.
    • Giveaways. Spin on a stream or a call so everyone sees the draw. Give extra entries by typing a name more than once.
    • Who goes first. Turn order, chores, who picks the movie.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is the wheel really random?

    Yes. The winner is picked with your browser's secure random number generator before the wheel starts moving, and every slice has the same chance. The spin animation just shows you where it landed.

    Can I remove the winner and spin again?

    Yes. Tap Remove and spin again. The winning slice comes off the wheel and it spins right away, so you can work through a whole class without repeats. Reset wheel puts every removed slice back.

    Can I give one entry better odds?

    Type it twice. Each line is its own slice, so an entry on the list twice gets two slices and twice the chance. The wheel never removes duplicates on its own.

    Can I share my wheel with someone?

    Yes. Tap Copy link to this wheel and send it. The link holds your entries, so whoever opens it gets the same wheel, ready to spin.

    Is my list uploaded anywhere?

    No. The wheel runs in your browser and nothing is saved. Close the tab and the list is gone. The only time your entries leave your device is when you copy a link and send it yourself.

    How many entries can I add?

    From 2 to 100. Past about 40 the slice labels get small, so for big lists the Random Name Picker is easier to read.
