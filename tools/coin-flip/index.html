How to Use the Online Coin Flip

Tap Flip Coin to get heads or tails. The tool keeps a running count of flips, heads and tails, plus a row of your recent results, so you can play best of three or keep score in a game. Reset Stats clears the count, and Copy Result copies the last flip so you can paste it into a chat.

Is a real coin flip 50/50?

Close, but not quite. In 2023 a team of researchers led by František Bartoš flipped coins 350,757 times and found that a coin lands on the same side it started on about 50.8% of the time. A slight wobble in the air keeps the starting face up a little longer. It's a small edge, but it's real.

This tool doesn't have that problem. There's no starting side, so every flip is an even 50/50 and doesn't depend on the flip before it.

Streaks are normal

Flip enough times and you'll see runs that look rigged. They aren't. Streaks show up far more often than most people expect:

  • In 10 flips, there's an 83% chance of at least 3 in a row on the same side.
  • In 20 flips, there's about a 46% chance of 5 in a row.
  • In 100 flips, there's an 81% chance of 6 in a row, and slightly better than even odds (54%) of 7.

A coin has no memory. After five heads, the next flip is still 50/50.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the coin flip truly random?

Yes. Each flip uses your browser's random number generator, which gives heads and tails an equal chance. Past flips have no effect on the next one.

Can I play best of three?

Yes. Keep flipping and watch the Heads and Tails counts. The first side to reach 2 wins a best of three, and 3 wins a best of five. Reset Stats starts a new round.

Why use an online coin flip instead of a real coin?

It's handy when you don't have a coin on you, when you're deciding something on your phone, or when you're on a video call and want everyone to see the same result. Share your screen and flip.

What can I use a coin flip for?

Anything with two options: who kicks off, who goes first in a board game, which of two restaurants, who takes the trash out. For more than two choices, the random name picker works the same way with a whole list.

Is this tool free?

Yes. There's no account and no limit on flips.
