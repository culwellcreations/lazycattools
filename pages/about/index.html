About Lazy Cat Tools

Lazy Cat Tools is a small collection of free browser tools: the kind of thing you search for, use for thirty seconds, and forget the tab is open. Split a class into teams. Pick a raffle winner. Settle an argument with a coin. Convert a recipe from cups to grams without doing math on the back of an envelope.

Who runs it

Hi, I'm Jake. I build and look after Lazy Cat Tools on my own, under the Culwell Creations name. I test every tool on a phone and a laptop before it goes live, and fix it when someone tells me something's off. There's no team, no investors, and no plan to start charging for anything.

Why it exists

Most "free" tool sites bury the tool under pop-ups, sign-up walls, and autoplay video. This one tries to do the opposite: the tool is the first thing on the page, it works without an account, and it does one job properly. If a tool needs more than a few seconds to figure out, that's treated as a bug.

How it works

Every tool runs in your browser. Whatever you type into a tool (names, lists, amounts) stays on your device and isn't sent to a server. The site does use Google Analytics to count visits and Google AdSense to show ads, which is how it stays free. The privacy policy explains exactly what those collect.

What's next

New tools are added based on what people actually need. The kitchen converters came first: cups to grams for specific ingredients, charts for common amounts, a recipe scaler, a converter that handles a whole recipe at once, and butter and oven temperature converters. If there's a conversion you keep looking up, tell me and it might be next.
