Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

Lazy Cat Tools does not ask for, store, or sell personal information. Every tool runs entirely in your browser: names, lists, amounts and anything else you type into a tool stay on your device and are never sent to our servers. The only data collected is what Google Analytics and Google AdSense gather, described below.

Advertising

This site displays ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

To learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for your consent before personalized ads or analytics cookies are used. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy link shown at the bottom of the page.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics (Google tag G-NBCK7DP1KN) to collect anonymous usage data such as pages visited, time on site, and general location (country/region). This data helps us understand how the tools are being used so we can improve them.

Google Analytics uses cookies and may collect your IP address. Google may process this data on servers outside your country. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Cookies

Lazy Cat Tools does not set any first-party cookies. Third-party cookies may be set by Google AdSense (advertising) and Google Analytics (usage tracking) as described above.

Third-party links

Some pages may link to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of this page will reflect that.

Children

These tools are general-audience tools and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page or to culwellcreations@gmail.com.
