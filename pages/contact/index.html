Contact

Usually answered within a few days.

The fastest way to reach Lazy Cat Tools is email:

culwellcreations@gmail.com

Good reasons to write

Something's broken. Tell me which tool, what you did, and what happened. The device and browser you were using helps a lot.

An answer looks wrong. If a conversion or result doesn't match what you expected, send the numbers you used. Corrections get fixed quickly and the page is updated.

You want a tool that doesn't exist. Most new tools start as someone's request. Describe what you were trying to do and what you searched for.

Anything else. Questions about privacy, ads, or using a tool in a classroom are all welcome.

What not to send

Please don't email passwords, payment details, or other private information. None of the tools need it, and I will never ask for it.
